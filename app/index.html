Backing track needs a SoundFont

Playing the band needs a General MIDI SoundFont. ukejam doesn't bundle one (the good banks aren't free to redistribute), so grab a free one — a one-time download.

Use a different SoundFont

Any General MIDI .sf2 works. Search the web or musical-artifacts.com for “GM SoundFont sf2”. Good free picks: GeneralUser GS, FluidR3_GM, MuseScore MS Basic.
