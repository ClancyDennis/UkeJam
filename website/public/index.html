Practice that actually listens.

ukejam is a play-along practice app for baritone ukulele. Load a song, follow the chord highway, and let the app hear what is really ringing — which target notes landed, which are missing, and which extras crept in.

Built for baritone ukulele (D-G-B-E tuning).

Timed chord highway

Chords scroll toward you in time with the song, with upcoming shapes shown clearly enough to play without reading the whole screen.

Live listening feedback

The microphone feeds a low-latency chord detector. ukejam compares the expected chord against the notes it hears and shows clean, missing, and extra notes as you play.

Built-in tuner

A fast, accurate tuner tuned for baritone ukulele, powered by the same real-time audio engine as the chord detector.

Import songs your way

Paste a tab or ChordPro chart, or import a MIDI file — ukejam derives a timed chord chart automatically and adds it to your library.

Backing tracks

Play along with MIDI backing rendered through a General MIDI SoundFont. Pick the instruments you want behind you — usually bass and drums.

Wait-for-me mode

Practicing a tricky passage? The song can wait until you actually play the chord before moving on.

AI coaching, on your device

Powered by Apple Intelligence, ukejam's built-in coach reviews how your practice went, points out the chords that need work, and cleans up messy imported tabs — all without your playing ever leaving your device.
