Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

The short version: ukejam does not collect, store, or share your personal data. Audio from your microphone is analyzed on your device in real time and is never recorded, saved, or transmitted. AI coaching runs on-device through Apple Intelligence by default; only if you connect your own AI provider (OpenRouter or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint) are song charts and practice summaries sent to that provider. Your song library lives only on your device.

Who we are

ukejam ("the app") is a play-along practice app for baritone ukulele. This policy describes how the app handles information when you use it on iOS. If you have any questions, contact us at cdeltainc@gmail.com.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Microphone access

ukejam requests microphone access so it can hear your instrument for the tuner and for live chord feedback. Here is exactly what happens with that audio:

  • Audio is processed on your device only, in real time, to estimate pitch and detect which notes are ringing.
  • Audio is never recorded, never written to disk, and never leaves your device.
  • Once a snippet of audio has been analyzed, it is discarded immediately.
  • You can revoke microphone access at any time in iOS Settings. The app remains usable without it — you just won't get live feedback or tuning.

AI coaching

ukejam's practice coach and tab cleanup are AI features. In every mode, the coach works from practice results the app already computed locally (which chords were clean, missing, or extra) and from song charts you imported — never from raw microphone audio, which stays on your device as described above. There are two ways to run it:

Default: Apple Intelligence, on-device

  • By default, coaching runs on your device through Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device foundation models. Nothing is sent to us or to any third-party AI service.
  • Apple Intelligence is an iOS system capability governed by Apple's own privacy protections and your device settings.

Optional: your own AI provider

You can instead connect the coach to an AI provider of your choice — OpenRouter or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — using your own account and API key. This is off unless you set it up, and if you do:

  • Coaching prompts (song charts and locally computed practice summaries) are sent directly from your device to the provider you configured. That provider's own privacy policy and data practices then apply to what you send — review them before connecting.
  • Your API key and endpoint settings are stored only on your device. We do not proxy, log, or have any access to your requests, your key, or the provider's responses.
  • You can disconnect the provider at any time in the app's settings, which returns coaching to on-device Apple Intelligence (or turns it off if your device doesn't support it).

Your song library

Songs you import (pasted tabs, ChordPro charts, or MIDI files) are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to any third party. Deleting the app deletes your library.

Optional downloads

Backing-track playback uses a General MIDI SoundFont, which is not bundled with the app. If you choose to use backing tracks, the app offers a one-time download of a freely redistributable SoundFont (GeneralUser GS) from its public hosting location. This request downloads a file to your device; it does not send any personal information beyond what any ordinary file download involves (such as your IP address, seen by the file host).

Data sharing and selling

We do not share or sell any data, because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It contains no ads, no external links to social media, and no in-app purchases of third-party content.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior ever changes in a way that affects your privacy — for example, if we add an optional online feature — we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above before that change ships.

Contact

Questions or concerns about privacy? Email cdeltainc@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.
