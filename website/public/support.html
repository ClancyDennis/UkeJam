Frequently asked questions

The app isn't hearing my ukulele. What should I check?

First, make sure microphone access is allowed: open iOS Settings → ukejam → Microphone. Then check that nothing is covering the mic and that you're in a reasonably quiet room. Strum firmly and let the chord ring — very light or heavily muted strums are harder to detect.

Chord detection gets confused while a backing track is playing.

Sound from the speaker bleeds into the microphone and contaminates detection. The reliable setup is headphones for the backing track, so the mic only hears your instrument. This is the recommended practice mode.

Why does the app say it needs a SoundFont for backing tracks?

Backing tracks are rendered from MIDI through a General MIDI SoundFont, and good GM banks aren't free to redistribute, so one isn't bundled. The first time you play a backing track, the app offers a one-tap download of GeneralUser GS (free, about 30 MB). Everything except backing playback works without it.

What tunings and instruments are supported?

Both common ukulele tunings: standard G-C-E-A (soprano, concert, and tenor) and baritone D-G-B-E. Pick yours on the Setup screen — the tuner, the chord detector, and the chord diagrams all follow it. Songs are stored instrument-agnostically as chord names, so the same song works on either instrument and the app shows the right shape for the tuning you chose.

How do I add a song?

Two ways: paste a chord tab or ChordPro chart into the import view, or import a MIDI file. MIDI imports get an automatic timed chord chart, which enables the scrolling chord highway and backing playback.

Where are my songs stored? Can I sync them between devices?

Your library is stored locally on your device and nowhere else. There is currently no sync or cloud backup — deleting the app deletes the library, so keep copies of source tabs or MIDI files you care about.

A chord name looks wrong on an imported MIDI song.

Chord names are derived from the notes in the MIDI file, and extended or ambiguous voicings (like A7 or Dmaj7) can come out simplified or named differently than the sheet music you know. If a song consistently imports incorrectly, email us the MIDI file and we'll take a look.

What do I need for the AI practice coach?

By default the coach and tab cleanup run on-device through Apple Intelligence, which needs a device and iOS version that support it (for example, iPhone 15 Pro or later on a recent iOS), with Apple Intelligence enabled in Settings. No supported device? You can connect your own AI provider instead (see below). Without either, coaching is unavailable but everything else — tuner, chord highway, live feedback, backing tracks — works normally.

Can I use my own AI model instead of Apple Intelligence?

Yes. In the app's settings you can point the coach at OpenRouter or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint using your own API key. The key stays on your device, and coaching prompts go directly from your device to the provider you chose — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what is sent. Usage is billed by your provider under your account.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Use the Email support button at the top of this page. For bugs, include what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead — screenshots or screen recordings help a lot.
